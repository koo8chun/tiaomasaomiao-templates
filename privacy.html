Barcode Miao — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Barcode Miao is a local tool that runs on your device. There is no account sign-in, and we do not upload your scan history, stocktake data, or generated codes to our servers.

The app uses the camera only to recognize barcodes and QR codes. Scanning is unavailable until you grant camera access.

The app may access your photo library to recognize codes from images and to save generated code images, only when you use those features.

When you import book quantities or export CSV, files are read or written under your action and shared through the system share sheet. File contents are not sent to our servers.

Scan records, stocktake data, generation history, and preferences stay in on-device storage. Uninstalling the app removes this data according to the system.

The app has no ads, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not track you across apps, and does not sell data to third parties.

The app is for general work and personal use and is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy is updated, this page and the in-app copy will be revised.
